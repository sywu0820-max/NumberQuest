# v0 math invariants

- Addition questions in 20/50/100 worlds never exceed the world's maximum.
- Subtraction never produces a negative answer.
- Multiplication factors are 1 through 9 only.
- Each question renders four distinct non-negative answer choices.
- A wrong attempt never reduces gems, XP, unlocked worlds, or best stars.
- Five correct answers clear a world and unlock the next world when applicable.
